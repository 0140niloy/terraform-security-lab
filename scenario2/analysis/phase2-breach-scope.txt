============================================================

SIMULATION ONLY — FORENSIC TRAINING EXERCISE

OBJECTIVE
---------

Determine the scope of potentially accessed PHI, determine whether data
actually left controlled systems, identify patient-safety implications,
and document the regulatory evidence required for a HIPAA assessment.


1. CURRENT EVIDENCE POSITION
------------------------------------------------------------

The synthetic evidence establishes:

- PHI database access by dba-senior-01;
- a simulated bulk query involving 125,000 rows;
- a simulated export operation;
- a simulated 500 GiB removable-media transfer attempt;
- DLP BLOCKED the removable-media transfer;
- an attempted external SFTP transfer was BLOCKED;
- attempted access to HR personnel records was BLOCKED.

The current evidence does NOT establish:

- exactly 125,000 unique patients were affected;
- that all queried records were copied;
- that 500 GiB of PHI left the organization;
- that the external SFTP destination received data;
- that any patient record was deleted;
- that any patient experienced harm.

7. POTENTIAL IMPACT CATEGORIES
------------------------------------------------------------

PHYSICAL HARM:

Requires evidence that clinical information was altered, deleted,
unavailable, or maliciously manipulated.

BLACKMAIL / EXPLOITATION:

Risk depends on whether sensitive categories such as mental-health,
substance-use, reproductive-health, genetic, or other highly sensitive
information were involved.

IDENTITY THEFT:

Risk depends on whether identifying and financial information was exposed.

INSURANCE FRAUD:

Risk depends on the presence of insurance identifiers, claims,
billing information, and other relevant records.

No category should be marked CONFIRMED until supported by record-level
evidence.
