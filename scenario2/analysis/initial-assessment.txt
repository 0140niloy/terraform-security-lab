SCENARIO 2 - INITIAL FORENSIC ASSESSMENT
=========================================

Potentially significant indicators:

1. Production database access during an unusual period.
2. Authentication through an unusual network/location.
3. Large transfer to removable media.
4. Attempted transfer to a non-approved external SFTP destination.
5. Modification of sensitive database audit configuration.
6. Lateral access toward HR systems.
7. Authentication activity from geographically inconsistent locations.

INTERPRETATION
--------------

The combination of these indicators represents a high-priority security
investigation.

However, the evidence does not by itself establish whether the legitimate
DBA performed the actions or whether an attacker controlled the DBA's
identity/workstation.

The investigation must establish:

A. WHO performed the actions?
B. WHICH device generated them?
C. HOW was authentication obtained?
D. WAS MFA legitimately completed, bypassed, or abused?
E. WAS the DBA physically present?
F. WHAT data was accessed?
G. WHAT data was actually copied?
H. WHERE did copied data go?
I. WAS there persistence or malware?
J. WAS the activity authorized?
K. DID another identity participate?
L. WAS audit evidence deleted or modified?

HIGH-VALUE CORRELATIONS
-----------------------

Correlate:
- Okta authentication
- MFA events
- VPN session
- EDR device telemetry
- Database sessions
- Database audit records
- USB/DLP events
- Network flows
- SFTP connection attempts
- HR-system access
- Change-management records

Do not infer intent solely from:
- Time of day
- Geographic location
- Public Wi-Fi
- Employee seniority
- Tenure
- Security-clearance history

Those characteristics are investigative context rather than proof of
malicious intent.
