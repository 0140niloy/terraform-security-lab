SCENARIO 2 - INITIAL INVESTIGATION HYPOTHESES
==============================================

Objective:
Determine whether unusual DBA activity represents:

H1 - MALICIOUS INSIDER
----------------------
The legitimate DBA intentionally performed unauthorized activities
for personal, financial, espionage, sabotage, or other purposes.

H2 - COMPROMISED ACCOUNT
------------------------
An external attacker obtained or bypassed the DBA's credentials and
used the legitimate account.

H3 - COMPROMISED DBA WORKSTATION
---------------------------------
Malware or another endpoint compromise caused the activity while the
DBA's identity and credentials were legitimately or illegitimately used.

H4 - OPERATIONAL ANOMALY
------------------------
The activity has a legitimate operational explanation, such as emergency
maintenance, approved data processing, VPN/proxy behavior, or a monitoring
artifact.

CURRENT STATUS
--------------
No hypothesis is confirmed.

Important:
Unusual location, unusual working hours, impossible-travel alerts, large
downloads, or access to sensitive systems are indicators requiring
investigation. They do not independently establish malicious intent.

Evidence required for determination:
- EDR telemetry
- Authentication and MFA records
- VPN/proxy logs
- Database audit records
- Network flow records
- Endpoint forensic evidence
- File-transfer evidence
- Change-management records
- Authorized administrative activity
- Credential/key history
- Relevant communications where legally authorized
