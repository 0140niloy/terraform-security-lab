SCENARIO 2 - INSIDER THREAT INVESTIGATION
=========================================

OBJECTIVE
---------
Determine whether the observed activity is consistent with intentional
unauthorized activity by the legitimate DBA.

AUTHORIZED EVIDENCE
-------------------
Review only evidence authorized by organizational policy, legal counsel,
HR, privacy requirements, and applicable law.

WORK ACTIVITY
-------------
Compare:
- Normal DBA working patterns
- Approved maintenance windows
- Change tickets
- Emergency maintenance
- Scheduled jobs
- Historical database-access patterns
- Previous administrative activity

COLLEAGUE / WITNESS INFORMATION
-------------------------------
Where authorized:
- Identify approved maintenance activity
- Validate whether the DBA was expected to work
- Establish whether another employee requested the activity
- Document observations without treating opinions as factual conclusions

COMMUNICATIONS
--------------
If legally authorized, investigate relevant organizational communications
for:
- Incident-related instructions
- Data-transfer requests
- Unauthorized access requests
- Coordination with other accounts

Do not access private communications without appropriate authorization.

FINANCIAL / PERSONAL INVESTIGATION
-----------------------------------
Any investigation of personal finances, foreign contacts, lifestyle
changes, or private activity requires appropriate legal and organizational
authorization.

Such information must not be treated as evidence of malicious intent
without corroborating technical evidence.

COLLATERAL INVESTIGATION
------------------------
Identify:
- Other accounts accessing the same data
- Other workstations involved
- Shared credentials
- Administrative accounts
- Service accounts
- Possible accomplices
- Additional data-transfer events

DARK-WEB / BREACH CHECKS
------------------------
If authorized:
- Search approved threat-intelligence sources
- Search known breach datasets
- Search for organizational indicators
- Preserve evidence and timestamps

Do not upload patient information, credentials, or confidential data to
public search services.

OBJECTIVE ASSESSMENT
--------------------
Compare evidence against competing hypotheses:

H1 - Malicious insider
H2 - Compromised account
H3 - Compromised workstation
H4 - Operational anomaly

Record:
- Supporting evidence
- Contradicting evidence
- Missing evidence
- Confidence level
- Alternative explanations

Do not make an attribution or intent determination until the evidence
supports it.
