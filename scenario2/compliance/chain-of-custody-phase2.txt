SCENARIO 2 — PHASE 2 FORENSIC CHAIN OF CUSTODY
============================================================

SIMULATION ONLY

CASE:
Hybrid Cloud Privileged-Account Security Investigation

EVIDENCE HANDLING PRINCIPLES
----------------------------

1. Preserve original evidence without modification.
2. Perform analysis only on forensic copies.
3. Record every evidence transfer.
4. Record acquisition date/time.
5. Record investigator identity.
6. Record evidence identifier.
7. Record cryptographic hash where applicable.
8. Record storage location.
9. Record access purpose.
10. Preserve original timestamps and metadata.

FORENSIC PRESERVATION
---------------------

Original drives:
Preserve using appropriate forensic acquisition procedures.

Memory:
Acquire volatile memory before shutdown where operationally and legally
appropriate.

Database:
Preserve native audit, transaction, recovery, and configuration logs.

Cloud:
Preserve relevant identity, control-plane, storage, and network logs.

Screenshots:
Preserve original files and associated metadata.

LEGAL HOLD
----------

Legal counsel should determine applicable preservation obligations and
scope of legal hold.

CRIMINAL REFERRAL
-----------------

Any referral to law enforcement should be coordinated through authorized
organizational leadership and legal counsel.

No attribution to a foreign government, criminal group, competitor, or
individual should be made without sufficient evidence.
