PHASE 3 - EVIDENCE CHAIN OF CUSTODY
===================================

Status:
Synthetic incident-response exercise. All cloud events and artifacts are simulated.

Evidence handling principles:
1. Preserve original evidence without modification.
2. Record SHA-256 hashes for collected evidence.
3. Maintain timestamps and source information.
4. Keep analysis copies separate from preserved originals.
5. Record every transformation or analysis performed.
6. Validate log integrity before relying on evidence for conclusions.
7. Preserve relevant CloudTrail, VPC Flow Logs, ALB/WAF logs, IAM history,
   S3 access logs, serverless configuration history, and CI/CD records.
8. Preserve evidence of possible log deletion or modification.
9. Document access to evidence for legal and forensic review.

Important limitation:
This exercise does not establish that a real-world breach occurred.
Synthetic evidence must not be treated as production cloud evidence.

Investigation status:
Encryption at rest is documented for the simulated customer-financial-data
bucket using a simulated AWS KMS key identifier. No simulated key material
or key-access events are present. In a real investigation, KMS Decrypt events,
key policies, grants, and CloudTrail records would require review.
